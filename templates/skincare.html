WHY Deep Cleansing?

Because our body accumulates foreign matter particles on skin such as:

Dust • ฝุ่น Grime • สิ่งสกปรก Stain • คราบ Sweat • เหงื่อ Dead Cells • เซลล์ตาย Oil • ความมัน Cosmetics • เครื่องสำอาง

These cause: Skin diseases, Pimples, Acne, Wrinkles, Dark circles, Bad smell, and even Skin cancer!

✨ We must WASH and REMOVE for healthy skin!

HOW? The 3-Step Method

1
First Step: HONEY SOAP
• Wet your skin and apply HONEY SOAP
• Massage for a moment
• Do it 2 TIMES - Don't rinse yet!
2
Second Step: TURMERIC SCRUB + HONEY SOAP
• Apply Turmeric Scrub together with HONEY SOAP
• Gently scrub & massage on Face and Body
• Rub around 1-3 minutes in the same direction
• Continue until dirt is removed
• ⚠️ Don't use too much water!
3
Third Step: HONEY SOAP AGAIN & RINSE
• Apply HONEY SOAP one more time
• Rinse thoroughly with water
• Pat dry gently

⏰ WHEN to do Deep Cleansing:

ONLY 1-2 TIME(S) MONTHLY
(Depending on skin conditions)
